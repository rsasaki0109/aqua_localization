# MBES-SLAM `beach_pond` Dead Reckoning (aqua_graph vs classical DVL DR)

Track B starts from the navigation prior that bathymetric SLAM corrects: how
well does the vehicle know where it is *before* any MBES loop closure? This
page measures it on the whole `beach_pond` survey (~2.4 h, ~16.7 km at
~2 m/s, surface vessel) against the dataset's RTK GNSS-INS reference
(`/nav/processed/odometry`). **GNSS position is never an input.** The only
GNSS-derived input is the dual-antenna heading, and only in the "with
compass" rows.

## What the numbers say

- **With a compass, aqua_graph beats classical DVL DR on absolute error**
  (RMSE 2.84 vs 3.60 m, max 5.91 vs 9.05 m over 16.7 km, i.e. 0.017 % of
  distance travelled), with identical segment drift (0.13-0.31 %). The gain
  comes from the IMU carrying the 1934 invalid DVL samples (no bottom lock)
  and from the coupled attitude / lever-arm model, not from a better
  velocity sensor. The online gyro-z bias settles at 3.1e-4 rad/s, matching
  an independent estimate from integrating the raw gyro against the compass
  (~3e-4 rad/s).
- **The Madgwick AHRS yaw is not a usable heading** here (~60 % segment
  drift): measured against the compass it drifts ~1 deg/min, so it is not
  an absolute heading on this platform.
- **Without a compass, yaw is unobservable** and both methods are bounded by
  gyro-bias yaw drift: aqua_graph matches integrated-gyro DR (2.31 vs
  2.37 m over the first 300 s; 19.1 vs 19.5 m over 20 min) and both reach
  hundreds of metres over 2.4 h. This is the regime MBES loop closures have
  to fix (next step).

## Findings on the way (all fixed or encoded in the profiles)

1. **DVL invalid sentinel.** The Nortek twist carries -32.75 m/s on all axes
   when bottom lock is lost (1934 of 68956 samples). aqua_graph's 5 m/s gate
   already dropped them; the classical baseline uses the same gate (without
   it, classical DR is off by kilometres).
2. **DVL starts late.** `/nav/processed/nortek/dvl_twist` begins ~853 s into
   the recording, so the scenarios start replay at 855 s.
3. **Moving-start gyro bias.** Accelerometer initialization used the mean
   gyro over the "static" window as gyro bias. The vessel is turning there,
   so the turn rate was frozen in as bias (-2.8e-3 rad/s, a linear 0.0034
   rad/s yaw drift once the compass is removed). New
   `init.gyro_bias_from_static_window: false` starts the bias at zero; the
   beach_pond profile uses it with GX5-25 turn-on bias priors.
4. **Gyro-z bias is wrongly observable without heading.** With IMU + DVL
   only, lateral specific force (a_y = w_z v_x + g roll) makes b_gz weakly
   observable, and sub-milliradian roll / accel-bias errors pull it to the
   wrong sign (6.4 % drift vs 2.0 % for plain gyro integration over 300 s).
   The no-compass scenario therefore holds the gyro bias at its calibrated
   value. A DVL time-offset sweep (`dvl.time_offset_s`, new) ruled out
   latency as the cause (best +0.1 s, marginal).
5. **Smoother failures no longer kill the node.** Long gauge-free runs can
   make the fixed-lag marginal singular, and GTSAM's
   `IncrementalFixedLagSmoother` can leave a marginalized key in the Bayes
   tree ("Requested variable 'x0' is not in this VectorValues"). aqua_graph
   now re-anchors a fresh smoother at the latest prediction and counts it in
   `GraphEstimatorStatus.smoother_resets` (0 in the compass run, 1 in the
   2.4 h no-compass run).

## Inputs and frames

- IMU: `/nav/sensors/microstrain/imu/raw` (3DM-GX5-25, 100 Hz, z-up
  `ustrain_imu`).
- DVL: `/nav/processed/nortek/dvl_twist` (frame `nortek_dvl`, ~7 Hz).
  `R_imu_dvl` = rpy (pi, 0, -pi/2) and lever arm (1.105, 0.093, -0.843) m in
  the IMU frame, both from the bag's `/tf_static`.
- Heading: `/nav/processed/navsat/ubx_hdg/imu`, the yaw of `gps_stbd`, whose
  x axis is parallel to the IMU x axis (measured: constant 91.86 deg to
  `base_link` yaw, std 0.1 deg).
- Depth: no pressure sensor on the surface vessel; aqua_graph holds the
  initial depth (`depth.hold_initial_depth`, sigma 0.2 m), classical DR pins
  z = 0.
- The DVL twist and heading are not in the primary converted bag; they live
  in `beach_pond_aux` (converted from the same ROS 1 bag) and are merged at
  replay time with `--extra-bag`.

Profiles: `aqua_graph/config/beach_pond.yaml`, scenarios
`aqua_replay/scenarios/beach_pond_graph.yaml` and
`beach_pond_graph_no_compass.yaml`, baseline
`aqua_replay/scripts/dvl_dead_reckoning_baseline.py`.
